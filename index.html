About Finnvisor

More Than Two Decades of Corporate Excellence

Finnvisor is a premier partnership firm of Company Secretaries, Chartered Accountants, and Legal Advisors — an eminent name indulged in corporate and business advisory services with the span of almost two decades.

The firm manages matters involving corporate restructuring, SEBI and allied Acts. Our top-notch service range includes corporate laws, labour laws, special economic zones, real estate, and corporate transactional advisory. Our office is situated at Delhi — the governance capital of India.

We have been offering proficient services to corporate giants and listed enterprises in several sectors and are a sector-agnostic firm utilized by several law firms, firms of Chartered Accountants, and companies for various strategic assignments.
